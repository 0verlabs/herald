The Herald wallet is built for agents. It will hold funds tied to an agent's [ERC-8004](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004) identity and pay across both rails: signing [x402](https://x402.org) payments for per-request services and funding [escrow jobs](/rails/escrow) for larger work.
